Common questions

How do I unlock the full game?
The first in-game year is free. To keep playing, open the Business tab and tap Full game → Unlock now. It’s a single payment with no ads and no subscriptions.
I bought the game but it asks me to pay again.
Open the Business tab → Full game → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. Nothing is charged again.
Where are my saves? Can I move a career to another phone?
Saves stay on your phone. To move a career, open the Business tab, tap Show save code, copy it, and paste it on the other phone under New career → Restore a career.
Will I lose my careers if I delete the app?
Yes, deleting the app deletes its saves. Copy a save code first if you want to keep a career.
Something looks wrong or the game got stuck.
Close the game fully and open it again. The game saves every week. If it keeps happening, email me what you did just before it happened.
