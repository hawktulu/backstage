Backstage: Band Manager

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Backstage does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game stays on your phone.

What the game stores

Your careers, settings and checkpoints are saved only on your device, in the app’s own storage. They never leave your phone unless you choose to copy a save code yourself, for example to move a career to another device.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • It does not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • It does not send your data to me or to anyone else.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your payment details. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by Apple’s own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds online features, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
